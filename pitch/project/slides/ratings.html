A ratings scale, on one published set of thresholds.

AAA is $10M out at under 2%; D is anything under $10k. 315 of 500 tokens are investment grade — BBB or better — and 8 are not rated at all.

The grade is deliberately arguable: one page of thresholds anyone can check by hand. A token loses a notch when one venue holds most of its volume, because depth on a single exchange is fragile depth. Recomputed daily, and a change between runs becomes the downgrade feed.
