Every token page shows its own evidence

The model, its error band, and the measurements that judge it.

The line is the estimate, shaded with its 50% band and dashed where it is extrapolating. The diamonds are what an immediate sale actually cost today.

When the books cannot absorb a sale, the page says the model is too optimistic at that size — on the token's own page.

Most models hide their misses. This one prints the contradiction next to the number, on the page a user is reading, in plain language. That is the trust argument — and it is also why the ratings are checkable by anyone with the same four public endpoints.
